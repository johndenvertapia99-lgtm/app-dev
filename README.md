# My Favorite Movie and Anime

<br>

## A Bronx Tale 🎬

<br>

**A Bronx Tale** is my favorite movie. It is a crime drama film released in 1993 and directed by Robert De Niro. The story follows a young boy named Calogero as he learns important life lessons from his father and a local mob boss named Sonny.

<br>

### Quick Facts

<br>

1. Released in 1993
2. Directed by Robert De Niro
3. Crime Drama Genre
4. Set in Bronx, New York

<br>

### Main Characters

<br>

- Calogero
- Sonny
- Lorenzo

<br>

### Why I Like It

<br>

- Teaches valuable life lessons
- Great storytelling
- Realistic characters
- Inspiring father-son relationship

## Black Clover 🍀

<br>

**Black Clover** is my favorite anime. It follows Asta, a boy born without magic who dreams of becoming the Wizard King.

<br>

### Quick Facts

<br>

1. Created by Yuki Tabata
2. First aired in 2017
3. Genre: Action and Fantasy

<br>

### Main Characters

<br>

- Asta
- Yuno
- Noelle Silva
- Yami Sukehiro
